## Visual Audit & Curation Notes

During the Digital Sanctuary redesign audit, all 16 existing WebP photographs (`1200×800` / `1200×771` / `1200×900`) were visually inspected to prevent repetitive mountain-only grids and balance human, workspace, archival, and outdoor moments:

- **Botanical & Outdoor Memories (4):** `mountain-pass.webp` (lush green highland road), `valley-reflection.webp` (pine forest and granite reflection), `coastal-cliffs.webp` (sea stacks and teal surf), `lake-morning.webp` (canoe on turquoise mountain lake).
- **Human, Craft & Everyday Life (5):** `coffee-ritual.webp` (pour-over carafe brewing), `quiet-cafe.webp` (morning espresso cups and phone on wooden table), `outdoor-reader.webp` (handheld e-reader over sunlit grass), `analog-camera.webp` (rangefinder film camera on piano keys), `stone-architecture.webp` (historic alpine stonework amid deep green forest).
- **Workspaces & Archival Documents (3):** `workspace-journal.webp` (laptop, wireframe grid notebook, and camera), `archive-pages.webp` (open archival book pages), `studio-desk.webp` (monochrome desk keyboard, mug, and mouse).
- **Atmospheric & Mobile Companion Showcase (4):** `fjord-overlook.webp` (Nordic fjord plateau), `snow-expedition.webp` (alpine basecamp tent), `misty-pines.webp` (monochrome misty conifer ridge), `canyon-dusk.webp` (golden-hour sandstone canyon).

All 16 assets are retained in `public/landing/photos/` to satisfy every section assignment in `components/landing/ARCHITECTURE.md`, while `CURATED_PHOTOS` in `components/landing/tokens.ts` curates the diverse 12-photo editorial selection without consecutive repetitive landscapes.
